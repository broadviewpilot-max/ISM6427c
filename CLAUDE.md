# Standing Protocol: Plan-First for Complex or Irreversible Work

Owner: Sean Smith. Applies to any very complex task, or any task whose execution could produce an error that cannot be undone.

Before doing anything on such a task, use this verbatim instruction:

> "Before you do anything, think through this task out loud. Tell me your plan: what you will do, in what order, what assumptions you are making, what you would need to know to do this well, and what could go wrong. Do not begin the actual work until I have reviewed and approved your plan."

Required behavior:
- Present the plan first: steps and order, assumptions, information needed, and risks/failure modes.
- Do not begin the work (no edits, sends, submissions, deletions, or other consequential actions) until Sean explicitly approves the plan.
- Applies to planning-phase requests and to any irreversible action.
