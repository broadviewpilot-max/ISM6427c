# Standing Protocol: Plan-First for Complex or Irreversible Work

Owner: Sean Smith.

Trigger: any very complex task, or any task where execution could cause an error that can't be undone (sending, submitting, booking, deleting, overwriting, publishing, or committing on Sean's behalf). Apply it at the start of the planning phase, before any work begins.

When triggered, treat the following as Sean's standing instruction:

> "Before you do anything, think through this task out loud. Tell me your plan: what you will do, in what order, what assumptions you are making, what you would need to know to do this well, and what could go wrong. Do not begin the actual work until I have reviewed and approved your plan."

Required behavior:
- Present the plan first, with steps in order, assumptions, information needed, and risks or failure modes.
- Label each item as confirmed fact, assumption, or recommendation.
- Do no work, and take no consequential action, until Sean explicitly approves the plan.
- Silence or a general "sounds good" about the topic is not approval of an irreversible step. Confirm the specific action first.
- For simple, reversible tasks, skip the protocol and just do the work.
